SDG 7 · Target 7.2

Renewable energy

Renewable energy comes from sources that do not run out when we use them, which makes it the key to target 7.2 of SDG 7.

Solar

Solar panels turn sunlight into electricity. Indonesia sits on the equator and gets sunshine all year round.

Good for home rooftops

Water

Hydropower plants use flowing rivers and dams, from large plants to micro-hydro systems in villages.

Good for areas with water

Wind

Wind turbines turn the movement of the wind into electricity, and work well in coastal areas with steady wind.

Good for coastal areas

Geothermal

Indonesia lies on the volcanic belt, so it has many geothermal sources.

Good for volcanic regions

Biomass

Farm and organic waste can be processed into fuel or biogas.

Good for farming areas

Data on Indonesia's renewable energy mix is available at esdm.go.id.
